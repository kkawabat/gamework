Live Demos

The same library, four ways to wire a session. Pick a connectivity and play.

Two-player peer-to-peer

Mesh, replicated. Both devices hold the same board and take turns writing moves. Losing the host would still leave a working connection — there is only one.

⭕
Tic-Tac-Toe
The plainest wiring: one role, one channel, nothing hidden.
🔴
Connect Four
Same mesh as tic-tac-toe. Drop pieces to get four in a row.
♔
Chess
Full movement, check and checkmate, still just two peers sharing a board.

N-player peer-to-peer

Mesh, authoritative. Every phone holds a channel to every other phone. The host deals in private — the odd one out is never sent the word.

🕵️
Odd One Out
Three to eight phones. Everyone gets a word except one player, who has to bluff.

Hub and spoke

Star, authoritative. Joiners dial only the host. Hole cards are a routing fact: each seat reads hand:{self} and nothing else private.

🃏
Poker
Texas Hold'em for 2–8. The host is the dealer; everyone else is a spoke.

One room, one hub

Star, authoritative, and never locked. Phones join all evening; the tally goes to whoever holds the asking seat and to nobody else, so nothing can bandwagon.

🤔
Would You Rather
An icebreaker for a room. Ask out loud, everyone picks a side, and the asking seat gets passed on.

Real-time

Mesh, authoritative — not for secrecy, for physics. Two devices simulating one bouncing ball drift apart within seconds. Paddle and ball ride an unreliable channel at 30Hz.

🏓
Tilt Pong
Two phones, one QR code. Tilt to move your paddle — the host phone runs the ball, both render themselves at the bottom.
